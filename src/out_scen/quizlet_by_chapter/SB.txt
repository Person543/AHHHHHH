You find a second account in /etc/passwd with UID 0, and another account whose hash in /etc/shadow starts with '!'. What does each mean?	UID 0 = root, so that second account is a hidden root backdoor (Linux authorizes by UID, not name). '!' in front of the hash = the account is locked.

You locked yourself out of a Linux box (lost root password) and separately find a SUID-root binary you don't recognize. What can you do with each?	Lost password: boot rescue/install media, mount the disk, chroot in, reset with passwd. Unknown SUID-root (find / -perm -4000): runs as root, so it's a privilege-escalation path or a planted backdoor shell.
