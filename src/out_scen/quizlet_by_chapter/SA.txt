Scenario: your lure is a tiny buffer overflow with almost no room for code, and later you phish a laptop on flaky hotel wifi that may drop after one connection. Which payload type for each, and why?	Tiny space -> STAGED (small stub downloads the rest). Flaky one-shot network -> STAGELESS (whole payload ships at once, needs no second connection).

You need to brute force one service and have limited time. The book says which targets/services are faster, and what 4 things define the attack?	Windows is faster than Linux, and SMB is faster than SSH (Windows Wins, SMB is Speedy). The 4 features: userlist, wordlist, time, defenses (Users Want Time Delays).
