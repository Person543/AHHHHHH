4 features of a brute force attack?	Userlist, wordlist, time it takes, defenses in the way.  [Hook: Users Want Time Delays.]
Shells on a Linux target?	You can get a plain Linux shell or Meterpreter. Upgrade the plain one with sessions -u.
2 non-Metasploit brute force tools?	NetExec and CrackMapExec. You can also write your own.
Where do wordlists come from?	Kali's built-in lists (rockyou.txt) or ones you generate yourself (crunch, cewl).
What stops a brute force attack?	Lockout, rate limiting, strong passwords, MFA, SSH keys only, alerts on failed logins.  [These are the 'defenses' feature.]
